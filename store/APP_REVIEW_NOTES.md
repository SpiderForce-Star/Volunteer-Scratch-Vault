## What this app is

Volunteer Scratch Vault is an independent remaining-prize **information**
tool for Tennessee scratch-off games. It ranks published remaining prizes
by heat (grand / mid-tier / bust). It is not a lottery, not a ticket
seller, and not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the
Tennessee Education Lottery Corporation.

Remaining counts do **not** improve the odds of winning any prize. Copy
says “highest remaining-prize heat,” never “best chance to win.”

Users must be 18 or older. The native app shows an age gate on first
launch. Problem-gambling help is in the footer and disclaimer:
1-800-GAMBLER and Tennessee REDLINE 1-800-889-9789.

## How to unlock Full Access

1. Confirm you are 18+ on first launch.
2. Open **Pricing**.
3. Start the monthly ($4.99) or annual ($49.99) free trial.
   Billing is App Store / Google Play (RevenueCat). There is no Stripe
   checkout in the iOS or Android binary.
4. **Account → Restore purchases** if the reviewer already subscribed
   on this Apple ID / Play account.
5. **Account → Manage subscription** opens the system subscription page.

Entitlement id: `vsv_full_access`  
Product ids: `monthly`, `annual`
